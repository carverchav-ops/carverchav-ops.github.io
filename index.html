About

A chorus without an LFO

Most chorus plugins wobble a delay line with a low-frequency oscillator. I led a plugin built on a different idea: get the thickness from the way several voices interact, like a group of tape machines running slightly out of sync.

  • Phase interactions between voices instead of one periodic sweep
  • Micro-pitch shifting for detune that moves naturally
  • Tape-style multi-voice modeling
