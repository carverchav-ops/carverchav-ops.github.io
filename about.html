About Me

Hi, I'm Carver. I'm a developer who builds free VST audio plugins in C++ with the JUCE framework.

For about three years I've been making plugins with a community of developers on Discord. That's where I learned JUCE and got into DSP and real-time audio.

I also make my own music, so I care about how a plugin sounds as much as how the code works.
